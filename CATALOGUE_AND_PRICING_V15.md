## Price calculation
- Metal price is read from the site's `/api/rates` response.
- Where the source doesn't publish a purity directly, 750 gold is derived from 916 by purity and 925 silver is derived from 999 by purity.
- Estimate = (current per-gram metal rate × estimated product weight) + (making charge per gram × estimated product weight) + an illustrative stone allowance where applicable.
- GST is excluded from every displayed catalogue estimate. GST is calculated separately at checkout/billing as configured.
- Weight, making charge and gemstone allowance are estimates only. Diamond and precious-stone pricing cannot be reliably determined from metal rates alone. Confirm exact weight, stone identity/grade, availability, making charge and final invoice with the shop before purchase.
- Prices are not official quotations and do not represent actual stock or a live inventory system.
